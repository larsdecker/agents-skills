# Source Ecosystem

For strategic or visibility audits, examine the third-party sources that appear for representative questions or are reported by provider data. Possible source types include reviews, publishers, forums, communities, reference sites, competitors, marketplaces, and specialist organizations.

Record the source URL, source type, observed topic/question, whether the target entity is present and accurately represented, evidence of use in AI answers, and a concrete gap or opportunity. Separate an observed citation from a hypothesis that the source influences answers.

Do not recommend outreach, reputation work, or placements solely because a source is prominent. Explain the relevance and whether the proposed action serves users or corrects missing/inaccurate information.
