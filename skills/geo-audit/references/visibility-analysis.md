# Observed AI Visibility

Use actual provider or analytics data where accessible. Record source, date range, market, platform, and definitions with every metric.

Useful measures may include citations, cited URLs, grounding queries, topics, intents, citation share, trend, referral visits, engagement, leads, and conversions. Availability and metric definitions differ across platforms; do not combine unlike measures as if they were equivalent.

Keep these signals distinct:

| Signal | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Citation | A source URL was attributed in an answer or platform report |
| Brand mention | The entity was named; no source attribution is implied |
| Recommendation | The answer endorsed an option in context |
| Click | A user followed a link |
| Conversion | A tracked business outcome occurred |

Manual prompt sampling can illustrate specific answers, but is sensitive to prompt wording, location, account state, time, and personalization. Label it as a sample, preserve prompts and dates, and do not generalize it into market-wide visibility. Citation counts do not establish rankings or causal business impact.

If reliable data is unavailable, say so. Do not estimate missing values.
