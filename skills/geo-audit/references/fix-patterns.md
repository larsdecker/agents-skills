# Fix Patterns for Citeability

Use these patterns only when they improve the answer, accuracy, or evidence. They are options for the author, not mechanical AI-writing rules.

## Make the subject explicit

**Before**
> Das führt dazu, dass Änderungen länger dauern.

**Possible revision**
> Fehlende Tests in häufig geänderten Modulen verlängern die Umsetzung.

Use when the reference is unclear outside the preceding paragraph. Keep a context-dependent sentence if the surrounding passage is needed for accuracy or style.

## Put the answer where readers can find it

**Before**
> Wir haben verschiedene Ansätze verglichen und uns nach längerer Diskussion für eine passende Lösung entschieden.

**Possible revision**
> Wir haben Rolling Wave Planning gewählt, weil sich der Projektumfang erst im Verlauf zuverlässig bestimmen lässt.

State the conclusion early when the page promises a direct answer. Do not remove useful narrative structure from a story or case study solely to front-load a claim.

## Add evidence and scope

**Before**
> Unsere Tests zeigten eine hohe Trefferquote.

**Possible revision**
> Im Test mit [Umfang und Version] erkannte das Verfahren [Ergebnis]. Der Test deckte [Grenzen] nicht ab.

Use only real, verifiable data. Never fill placeholders with invented numbers or imply that one test establishes universal performance.

## Choose a heading that matches the section

**Before**
> ## Vorteile

**Possible revision**
> ## Für welche Arbeitsabläufe eignet sich das Werkzeug?

Use question headings where they state the actual question answered. A clear descriptive heading is equally suitable when it fits the content better.

## Structure comparisons when helpful

Use a table when readers need to compare the same attributes across options. Keep meaningful qualifications and context; do not force narrative content into a table merely for extraction.

## Clarify entity attribution

Name the author or organization visibly and explain relevant experience. Structured data may represent those visible facts, but does not create expertise or verify a claim. Link only to genuine profiles and keep identifiers consistent where appropriate.

## When not to rewrite

- The text is intentionally narrative or experiential.
- A stylistic change would add no evidence or clarity.
- Splitting a sentence would reduce precision.
- The passage is a direct quotation or must preserve legal/technical wording.
